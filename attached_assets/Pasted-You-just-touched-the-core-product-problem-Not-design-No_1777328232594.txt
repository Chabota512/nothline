## Not:

“Log everything manually whenever you want”

## Not:

# 🔥 Best Logging System (Gold Standard)

# 1. Smart Intervals (Primary Method)

Yes, notifications are powerful—but not dumb fixed reminders.

Instead:

## Adaptive prompts

Examples:

* Every 45 mins during work hours
* Every 90 mins during weekends
* More prompts during active hours
* Less prompts during sleep/evening

Notification says:

> What have you been doing this past hour?

Tap options:

* Working
* Studying
* Social media
* Travel
* Break
* Meeting
* Other

Then optional note.

3-second logging.

---

# Why Not Every 15 Minutes?

Too aggressive.

15-minute reminders become:

* annoying
* ignored
* disabled
* hated

Use 15-minute **timeline blocks internally**, but prompts less frequently.

---

# Best Cadence

## Suggested Default:

### Deep workers / students:

45 mins

### Normal users:

60 mins

### Casual users:

90 mins

Let users choose.

---

# 2. Lock Screen Logging (Very Smart)

Yes — absolutely.

Notification on lockscreen:

> What were you doing from 10:00–11:00?

Buttons:

* Focused Work
* Distracted
* Break
* Custom

This reduces friction massively.

---

# 3. Missing Gap Recovery (Brilliant Feature)

If no logs for 3 hours:

At 6pm app asks:

> Help reconstruct your afternoon.

Then show timeline gaps:

13:00–16:00

User fills blocks quickly.

This solves real life.

---

# 4. Passive Signals (Future Premium Feature)

Use phone signals (with permission):

* movement
* screen time
* app usage
* location categories

Then suggest:

> Looks like you were commuting around 08:30.

User confirms.

Powerful.

---

# 5. Evening Reflection (Truth Layer)

Evening should not be raw logging.

Evening should be reflection.

Ask:

* What stole time today?
* Best use of time?
* Energy rating?
* Were logs accurate?

This is smarter than asking them to rebuild the whole day.

---

# 🧠 Product Psychology

Users hate chores.

So logging must feel like:

* tiny check-ins
* not homework
* no guilt
* no complexity

---

# 🔥 Founder-Level Strategy

Use a 3-tier system:

## Tier 1: Capture

Tiny prompts through day

## Tier 2: Repair

Fill missing gaps later

## Tier 3: Understand

Insights at end of day/week

That’s elite product design.

---

# ⚠️ Important Warning

Never make notifications “strong” by force.

Users uninstall apps that feel controlling.

Instead make them feel useful.

Bad:

> LOG YOUR TIME NOW

Good:

> Protect your day — quick check-in?

Bad:

> You missed your log.

Good:

> Want help remembering your afternoon?
